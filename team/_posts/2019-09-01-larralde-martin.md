Martin Larralde was a PhD student in the lab from 2019-09-01 to 2026-10-31, where he worked on discovering biosynthetic gene clusters in the gut microbiome and developing robust software. He is now a postdoctoral researcher in Lausanne, CH, working on human-centered health AI.
